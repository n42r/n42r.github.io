15 years where systems, products, and teams intersect — from enterprise platforms to EU research consortia to building  production software with AI agents. I work at the boundary between "what should we build" and "does it actually run," and I write about what I learn along the way. Architecture, leadership, and hands-on building — usually at least two of those at once.

Currently focused on AI-assisted software engineering: how architecture, team practices, and quality standards change when AI writes most of the code. I designed and tested a methodology for this by shipping a production product solo in 6 weeks — now writing about the approach and helping engineering teams adopt it.
