# Nasr Kasrin, Dr. rer. nat.

I help organizations design data architectures that work across organizational boundaries — the problem space now operationalized as Gaia-X, Catena-X, and the EU Data Act.

15 years across enterprise platforms (PUMA), EU-funded R&D consortia, and early-stage startups. My PhD (Bamberg, 2023) is on federated inter-organizational data sharing, grounded in six years of field work inside a multi-partner EU consortium. I work where systems, data, and teams intersect: turning ambiguous technical landscapes into structures organizations can execute against.

## What I work on

**Federated and inter-organizational data architecture.** Designing systems for data sharing across organizations with different infrastructures, legal scopes, and conventions. Adjacent to the data-spaces movement and EU Data Act obligations.

**Solution architecture for production systems.** Senior architect work for engineering organizations: tech-debt programs, reliability and incident practice, technical roadmaps that survive contact with delivery, AI/LLM tooling integration.

**Early-stage technical strategy.** Architecture and strategy advice for founders moving from prototype to production.

## Selected work

- [**SIMUTOOL**](https://github.com/simutool) — €3.5M EU aerospace SaaS for inter-organizational data management.
